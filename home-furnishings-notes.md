## Round rug for the dining table

- The table is 110 cm with 4 chairs.
- The rug should extend 60–70 cm past the table on every side, so the chairs stay on it when pulled out: 110 + 2 × 60–70 = **230–250 cm**.
- **Buy: 240 cm diameter** (a standard size). 200 cm is the absolute minimum, and the chairs will slide off its edge when pulled out.

## LED strip (too bright and too cold)

Goal: about 70% dimmer and warmer, with 1.5 cm strips over the LEDs.

- **Most precise option:** layer two lighting gels, cut into 1.5 cm strips and held on with clear tape:
  - LEE 204 Full CTO: warms daylight-white to about 3200K and lets through ~55%
  - LEE 209 0.3 ND: lets through 50%, with no colour change
  - Together they let through about 28%, so the strip ends up **about 70% dimmer**
- **Easiest option:** LightDims self-adhesive dimming sheet, cut into strips. Users report it roughly halves the brightness and warms the colour; add a second layer if that's not dim enough.
- **Alternative:** if the strip is a 12/24V strip, an inline dimmer dims it more cleanly. A 1/2 CTO gel would then be enough for the warmth.
