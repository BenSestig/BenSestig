## Bulbs: Philips SceneSwitch

**Dim with the existing light switch.** Turn it off and on quickly to step through 3 settings:

| Setting | Brightness | Colour |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 100% | 2700K warm white |
| 2 | ~40% | 2500K |
| 3 | ~10% | 2200K candle-like |

No dimmer, app or remote needed.

## Rugs

### Round rug for the dining table: Ø 240 cm

- The table is 110 cm with 4 chairs. The rug should extend 60–70 cm past the table on every side → 230–250 cm.
- **Pick a flat weave or low pile**, so the chairs slide easily.
- **Trendcarpet.es:** Ø 240 rugs from about €60. Listed "from" prices may be for smaller sizes, so check the price with 240 selected.
- **Leroy Merlin:** jute-look round rug Ø 200 cm, €95. That's the minimum size.
- **Nordic Nest:** premium Ø 240 wool rugs, €500+.

## LED strip (too bright and too cold)

Goal: about 70% dimmer and warmer, with 1.5 cm strips over the LEDs.

- **LEE 204 Full CTO**: warms the light and lets through ~55%. **Thomann.es** sells a 25 × 122 cm sheet for €6.30, currently "on request" (no confirmed delivery date). One sheet gives ~16 strips of 122 cm, about 19 m in total.
- **LEE 209 0.3 ND**: lets through 50%. Also a Thomann item; check the price.
- Together they let through ~28%, so the strip ends up **about 70% dimmer**. Hold them on with clear tape.
- If the strip is 12/24V, an inline dimmer plus a 1/2 CTO gel is an alternative.
