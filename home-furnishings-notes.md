## Rug

- It goes under the **dining table**: 110 cm, with 4 chairs.
- Rule of thumb: the rug should extend 60–70 cm past the table edge on every side, so the chairs stay on it when pulled out.
- **Recommended:** about 240 cm, either round, or square/rectangular to match the table's shape. 200 cm is the smallest that works, and the back chair legs may slip off when the chairs are pulled out.
- The earlier figures (160 wide, 180 max, 150 table + 20 cm) are too small for a dining rug with chairs.
