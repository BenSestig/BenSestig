### Mirror lamps: candle light, low glare

- **250–320 lm per bulb** is the old 25–40W candle level, normal for wall lamps next to a mirror.
- **What feels "super bright" is glare:** a bare clear filament at eye level, doubled by its reflection in the mirror. The gold-tinted glass and 2200K colour soften that.
- The Barcelonaled bulb gives a bit more light than SOLHETTA (320 vs 250 lm).
- **Fallback if it's too bright:** IKEA SOLHETTA E14 250 lm chandelier **opal** (305.840.34), €2.49 per 2-pack. Soft, but normal 2700K warm white rather than candle light.
- **IKEA's own candle-light option:** LUNNOM E14 210 lm, brown glass (605.392.38). 2200K, €3.99 each (~€28 for 7).

### Spots: why 230 lm

- **Total light:** 12 × 230 lm ≈ 2,800 lm, close to what the 6 working spots give today (if the old bulbs are the usual ~350 lm). 12 × 345 lm would be roughly double.
- **Tip:** read the lumens printed on an old bulb. Half that per bulb across 12 spots keeps today's brightness.

## Dimmer: ask the landlord

- **Ask for:** LED dimmers ("regulador LED") on the dining table lamp's switch and, optionally, the spots' switch.
- **When a dimmer goes in, swap to dimmable bulbs on that circuit:**
  - Spots: SOLHETTA GU10 345 lm dimmable, art. 205.840.96, €1.99 each (~€26 for 13).
  - Dining lamp: LUNNOM E27 470 lm dimmable (see above).
  - Non-dimmable bulbs on a dimmer flicker or don't switch off properly.
- **The dimmer must be:**
  - LED-compatible (trailing edge).
  - Rated for a **very low minimum load**. The dining lamp draws only ~7.5W (3 × 2.5W) and the spots ~30W. Below a dimmer's minimum load, LEDs flicker.
  - 2-wire (no neutral) if the switch box has no neutral wire.
  - The same brand and series as the existing switches, so it fits the frame. Examples: Simon 31 or Niessen Zenit, ~€56–57 each at Obramat.
- **Harder case:** a light that can be switched from two places (two-way / "conmutada").
- Use the same bulb model on each dimmer.

| Bulbs: 13 × dimmable SOLHETTA GU10 345 lm (€25.87) + 3 × LUNNOM E27 (€14.97) + the non-dimmable rest (E27 2-pack €2.99, E14 €12.45) | ~€56 |
| **Total** | **~€320–400** |

- **If the landlord pays** for the dimmers and the electrician, the cost is just the bulbs: **~€56** (vs ~€36 without dimmers).
- **Buy the dimmable bulbs for those circuits straight away.** Don't buy the non-dimmable GU10 230 lm or E27 250 lm for them.
- **Minimum load:** each dimmer carries only a few bulbs (e.g. ~4 spots ≈ 10–12W, the dining lamp ≈ 7.5W). Check that the dimmer's minimum LED load is that low.
- **Two-way switches:** if two of the 4 switches control the same lights, that circuit needs a different setup.

## LED strip (too bright and too cold)

Goal: about 70% dimmer and warmer, with 1.5 cm strips over the LEDs.

**Recommendation: lighting gel.** This is the heat-proof filter film used on stage and studio lights, cut into 1.5 cm strips and laid over the LEDs.

| Gel | What it does | Where | Price |
|---|---|---|---|
| **LEE 204 Full CTO** (orange) | Warms cold white to ~3200K; lets through ~55% | Thomann.es, 25 × 122 cm sheet | €6.30 (currently "on request") |
| **LEE 209 0.3 ND** (grey) | Lets through 50%, no colour change | Thomann or any photo/theatre shop | Check the price |

- **Result:** together they let ~28% through, so the strip ends up **~70% dimmer and warm white**.
- **Coverage:** one sheet of each gives ~16 strips of 1.5 × 122 cm, about 19 m of LED strip.
- **How:** cut strips the width of standard tape (~1.5–1.9 cm) with a ruler and a cutter. Lay a 204 and a 209 strip on top of each other over the LEDs, and fix only the ends with ordinary clear tape.
- **Is covering it safe?** Yes, for a normal LED strip.
  - It gets warm, not hot, so gel and tape won't burn. LEE gel is made for hot stage lights.
  - **Touch test:** after an hour on, if you can keep a finger on the strip, covering it is fine. If it's too hot to touch, don't cover it; use a dimmer instead.
  - Don't cover the strip's power adapter.
  - Ordinary clear tape can yellow and get sticky from the warmth over time (residue in a rental), so only tape the ends.
- **Same product, other brand:** Rosco calls it "Full CTO" (Rosco 3407 / E-Colour 204).
- **One-product variant:** two layers of LEE 204 and no 209. Also ~70% dimmer, but candle-warm (~2100K) instead of warm white. One sheet covers ~9 m.
- **Quick test first:** a roll of 15 mm cream washi tape (a few euros at Flying Tiger or Action). It dims and softens the light; add a second layer if it's not enough. Less precise and a bit "papery", but it peels off without residue (rental-friendly).
- **If the strip is 12/24V with an accessible plug:** an inline dimmer dims it properly. A single 1/2 CTO gel (LEE 205) then handles the warmth.
